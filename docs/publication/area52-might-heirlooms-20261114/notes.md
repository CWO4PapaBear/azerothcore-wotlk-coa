# Fused Quel'Serrar and Dusty Draconian Deflector
Two Strength and Stamina heirlooms join the collection and the existing sword and shield vendors. Fused Quel'Serrar uses level-scaled hit rating, its original icon and a flame-free blade. Its Fused Sanctuary grants defense rating and armor while equipped, scaling to 27 defense rating and 400 armor at level 80. Dusty Draconian Deflector uses the original shield appearance. Collection prices are 1,000 and 500 Runes respectively.
