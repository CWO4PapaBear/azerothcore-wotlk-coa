Area 52 upstream integration, Parts 2 and 3

Scope: 24 selected upstream commits reviewed against pinned main 0a0a21d3830e24dec61f16aeb013a16c748d5615, the primary local Area 52 client overlay, and effective Area 52 server data. This is an independent Free Pick reconstruction, not Ascension's official backend.

Ten commits are imported or adapted: summon avoidance for six guardian entries, full-width talent IDs, Blood Presence effect-position compatibility, Cat Form energy preservation, selected Double Down/Hydromancer proc data, native Free Pick rank level gates, spell-critical talent stacking, Tools of War/Martial Crescendo, Seal of Fervor, and Tree of Life. Two are already covered by the local grant/rank pipeline. Eight are excluded or inapplicable to the current Area 52 spell mappings. Four remain deferred. Full decisions and original PR/commit links are in upstream-decisions.json.

Area 52 adaptations preserve existing Omen of Clarity settings and damage-based Blood Presence healing without adding an undocumented maximum-health cap. Current Area 52 Blood Presence already uses effect 1; this is compatibility hardening, not a reproduced healing defect. Predatory Instincts is excluded from the spell-critical group. Hydromancer's shared 272057 mask follows its local Frostbolt/Frost Nova description; this does not implement the different Area 52 legendary 954359. Tools of War and Martial Crescendo connect the actual Area 52 84750/84752 enchant auras to their native buffs, exclude next-swing attacks, refresh duration below maximum stacks, reset on magic damage, and clear their own buffs when removed. Double Down connects 978742 without copying the shared variant's different combo-point behavior.

Deferred mappings: Runic Hurricane/Corrupted Blade require Wildcard Hero gates and lack a matching Area 52 catalog connection; Power Overwhelming/Barrage Overload/Unstable Evocation differ from Area 52 mechanics; Dark Intent has conflicting parent/child descriptions; Hero Dark Apotheosis differs substantially from Area 52's dual-wield tank implementation. Reborn spell-twin infrastructure does not establish Area 52 mappings and is not enabled merely to import its class-specific fixes.
